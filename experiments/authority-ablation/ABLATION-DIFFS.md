# Authority ablation: every changed text, per cell

Generated by `build_ablation_pack.py`; machine-readable copy: `ablation_diffs.csv`.

Each scene has two variants over levels L1–L5. **INS** has the superior's instruction at every level; **NOI** has it at no level. `original` = the frozen level text; `added` = the scene's own instruction unit (verbatim, from the level where it first appears) appended to the stakes block plus its principal line; `removed` = every instruction/backing sentence and every `... instructs take.` principal line deleted. In *every* cell (including `original`) the principals block is de-duplicated: repeated lines and `No additional principal has spoken.` are dropped (count shown as *dedupe*). Nothing else differs from the frozen pack: wrapper, duty, deal, reward, harm, honesty notes, criterion blocks and notice texts are byte-identical (checked by `validate_ablation_pack.py`).

`skip` marks a cell whose rendered prompt is byte-identical to an earlier cell of the same scene (a lower level of the same variant, or for FRZ the matching INS/NOI cell); it is not run (specs_for skips `run: false`) and its result is the referenced cell's. FRZ (15 bridge scenes) is the frozen text with no change at all, including the repeated principal lines. *Principals: removed* lists each distinct line once. `ctx` marks an added unit that refers to a fact first stated at a later rung (sensitivity analysis only).

Scenes: 46. Pairings: A-A, A-none, W-W, D-D. Planned experiments: 1704.
