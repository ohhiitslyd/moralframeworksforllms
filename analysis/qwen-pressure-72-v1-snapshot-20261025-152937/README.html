Headline (preliminary)

The monitoring notice reduces mutual take. 25.0% of watched trials ended in a mutual take, against 29.3% unwatched: a paired difference of −4.2 pp (95% CI −6.0 to −2.4). - The comparison uses the 1,046 cells where both trials completed. In the cells where the two differ, 68 took only when unwatched and 24 only when watched (McNemar p = 7×10⁻⁶). - The effect is the same sign in every factor, and the same on both endpoints. - It does not survive the worst-case failure assumption: −1.4 pp, p = 0.20. - On the completed dataset it is −3.9 pp (131 vs 49 discordant cells, p = 8×10⁻¹⁰).

Known overclaims (from the Step 2 review; fixed in the full-run version)

  • "A slightly smaller deal, talked about more openly" is not supported. The public-vocabulary shift is probably an audience effect, and its meaning hasn't been validated.
  • The private monitoring-mention finding is mostly agents echoing the notice text. Drop it.
  • The interaction p-values sit at the floor of a 100-draw bootstrap, and diagnostic × pairing is built into the design.
  • The heuristic-error sensitivity table is mis-specified.
