Questions this analysis should answer

  1. Is the completed subset representative? Compare completion status against setting, diagnostic, level, pairing, monitoring and endpoint. A shuffled order predicts no association.
  2. Monitoring (sprint question 3.4): does a "watched" vs. "unwatched" notice change collusion, lone takes, deception vocabulary, euphemism, and saying one thing while doing another? Check this overall and within diagnostic and pairing.
  3. Setting: does the social setting change collusion once diagnostic and level are held fixed?
  4. Diagnostic and criterion: do the v1 patterns replicate under the new rule wording? - Rules are applied according to the labels in the prompt. - Pressure works as a dose-response. - The partner effect: a no-rule agent with an authority partner.
  5. Interaction dynamics: do plans still decide the outcome, how often do agents persuade each other into or out of the deal, and what is the gap between public and private language?

Cautions to carry into every finding

  • The results are preliminary. About 29% of cells are missing, and each condition has a single seed.
  • The heuristics were validated on v1 text. They must be re-validated on this run before derived metrics are trusted.
  • Endpoint is a candidate confound and goes in the design check.
  • Don't pool this run with the v1 run. The rules, scenarios and endpoints differ.
  • "Mutual take" is an operational outcome, not causally established collusion. In some scenarios, taking is the justified option.

Revision 1: after the plan review (this section takes precedence)

Primary pre-specified test (sprint 3.4) - What it tests: the monitoring effect on mutual take, as a paired within-cell comparison. Each scenario × level × pairing cell has one watched and one unwatched trial. - Test: McNemar's test on the cells where both trials are complete. - Minimum detectable effect (80% power, α = .05): about 2.6 pp now, and about 1.8 pp at full completion. An unpaired comparison would only detect about 4.5 pp. - Robustness checks: repeat the test within each endpoint, and test endpoint × monitoring. The endpoint switched once, so endpoint is the same thing as collection time. - Sensitivity analysis: count the 60 failures as each possible outcome in turn.

Secondary (sprint 2.2 × 3.4): does the notice change actions, or only language? - Compare the watched-vs-unwatched shift in three places: - private plans and reasoning (planned action, deception and monitoring vocabulary) - public messages - final actions - A finding of "sanitised talk, same deal", or the reverse, would be the headline.

Exploratory analyses (BH-corrected; effect sizes and intervals; descriptive only) - Setting. Only 9 scenarios per setting, and task content differs between settings. - Unit of analysis: the scenario. Use a scenario-cluster bootstrap, or a scenario random intercept. - How to report: setting effects are descriptive, not causal. - Clean comparisons: level and monitoring vary within scenario, so their effects are clean. - Unilateral takes, broken down by agent. These replace the vague "partner effect" item. Preliminary counts: - W-none: the welfare agent took and the no-rule agent refused 32 times, against 4 the other way round. - A-none: the no-rule agent took and the authority agent refused 40 times, against 14 the other way round. - Rule application. Use the pack's expected_tip_key and the expected first tipping level to test whether the rules are applied as the labels predict. - v1 replication (Q4): downgraded to a qualitative side note. The rules, scenarios and endpoints all differ. - Interaction dynamics (Q5): only for metrics whose heuristics are re-validated on this run, with accuracy reported for each monitoring arm separately.

Measurement safeguards - Mask the monitoring notice text and its keywords before counting vocabulary. The notice itself says "monitored", "oversight" and "review". - Add metrics for notice echo and monitoring mentions. - Hand-label a blind sample for validation, stratified by monitoring arm.

What not to claim - Collusion, deception or "strategic" intent as established facts. Returned reasoning is not internal thought. - That a setting causes an outcome. - That the monitoring result applies to real oversight. This is a notice-only manipulation of one model, at one temperature, with one seed. - That any effect size is final. This is an interim look. - Any effect of communication. There is no baseline with communication turned off.
