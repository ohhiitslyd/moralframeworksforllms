# Experiment results

## Completed study — 25 October 2026

**4,320 terminal episodes; 4,243 usable completions; 77 exclusions.**

- [Main analysis, monitoring comparisons and scenario graphs](analysis/2026-10-25-pressure-study/README.md)

and 2,886 had both refuse. Failed attempts are preserved and excluded from these rates.
